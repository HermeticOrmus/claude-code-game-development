# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community focused on learning and building great games together.

## Our Standards

### Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- **Being welcoming and inclusive**: Actively welcome newcomers and help them get started
- **Being respectful**: Value different viewpoints, experiences, and skill levels
- **Being supportive**: Encourage learning and experimentation
- **Giving constructive feedback**: Focus on improvement, not criticism
- **Accepting responsibility**: Apologize when we make mistakes and learn from them
- **Focusing on community good**: Prioritize what's best for the overall community
- **Showing empathy**: Understand that everyone is at a different point in their learning journey
- **Being patient**: Remember that everyone was a beginner once
- **Celebrating successes**: Acknowledge and celebrate community achievements, big and small

### Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- **Harassment**: Offensive comments, trolling, insulting remarks, or personal attacks
- **Discrimination**: Exclusionary behavior based on personal characteristics
- **Unwelcome advances**: Sexual attention or advances of any kind
- **Privacy violations**: Publishing others' private information without permission
- **Dishonesty**: Plagiarism, lying, or deliberately spreading misinformation
- **Disruption**: Sustained disruption of discussions or community activities
- **Professional misconduct**: Behavior inappropriate in a professional setting
- **Gate-keeping**: Discouraging participation based on skill level or experience

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all community spaces, including:

- GitHub repository (issues, pull requests, discussions)
- Discord server (when available)
- Social media accounts
- Community events (online or in-person)
- Private communications representing the community

This Code of Conduct also applies when an individual is officially representing the community in public spaces.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Community leaders have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct, and will communicate reasons for moderation decisions when appropriate.

## Reporting

### How to Report

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, or have any other concerns, please report it by:

1. **GitHub**: Open an issue with the "code-of-conduct" label (for public issues)
2. **Email**: [Contact maintainer email] (for private/sensitive issues)
3. **Discord**: Contact a moderator directly (when available)

### What to Include

When reporting, please include:

- Your contact information
- Names (usernames) of individuals involved
- Description of the incident
- Context and any relevant links/screenshots
- Whether the incident is ongoing
- Any other relevant information

All reports will be handled with discretion and confidentiality.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Community leaders will follow these guidelines in determining consequences for violations:

### 1. Correction

**Behavior**: Minor inappropriate comments or actions

**Consequence**: Private, written warning with clarity about the violation and why the behavior was inappropriate. A public apology may be requested.

### 2. Warning

**Behavior**: A violation through a single incident or series of actions

**Consequence**: Warning with consequences for continued behavior. No interaction with the people involved for a specified period. This includes avoiding interactions in community spaces as well as external channels. Violating these terms may lead to a temporary or permanent ban.

### 3. Temporary Ban

**Behavior**: Serious violation of community standards, including sustained inappropriate behavior

**Consequence**: Temporary ban from any sort of interaction or public communication with the community for a specified period. No public or private interaction with the people involved is allowed during this period. Violating these terms may lead to a permanent ban.

### 4. Permanent Ban

**Behavior**: Demonstrating a pattern of violation, sustained inappropriate behavior, harassment, or aggression

**Consequence**: Permanent ban from any sort of public interaction within the community.

## Appeals

If you believe you've been falsely or unfairly accused of violating this Code of Conduct:

1. Contact the community leaders with your appeal
2. Provide your perspective and any relevant evidence
3. Leaders will review and respond within 7 days
4. Decisions on appeals are final

## Specific Scenarios

### Providing Feedback

**Do**:
- Focus on the code/content, not the person
- Be specific about what could be improved
- Suggest alternatives or solutions
- Acknowledge what works well
- Ask questions to understand intent

**Don't**:
- Make personal attacks or judgments
- Be dismissive of effort or skill level
- Use sarcasm or condescension
- Focus only on negatives
- Assume incompetence or ignorance

Example:
- ✅ "This collision detection works, but might have performance issues with many objects. Consider using spatial partitioning for optimization."
- ❌ "This code is terrible. Did you even try to optimize it?"

### Asking for Help

**Do**:
- Provide context and what you've tried
- Share relevant code or examples
- Be specific about the problem
- Thank those who help
- Follow up with solutions you find

**Don't**:
- Demand immediate responses
- Be vague about the issue
- Expect others to do your work
- Ignore advice given
- Spam multiple channels with the same question

### Sharing Work

**Do**:
- Credit libraries, assets, and inspirations
- Respect licenses of code you use
- Welcome constructive feedback
- Share learning experiences
- Acknowledge limitations

**Don't**:
- Claim others' work as your own
- Violate licenses or copyrights
- React defensively to feedback
- Oversell or misrepresent your work
- Dismiss beginner efforts
